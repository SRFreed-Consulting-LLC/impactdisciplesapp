Welcome to the Impact Disciples Event App! Find Instructions below on how to add this app to your home screen for quick access!
Safari
1. Click the Share button
The share button is typically located at the bottom of the screen. Look for the icon pictured above.
2. Add to Home Screen
The list of share options will have an 'Add to Home Screen' button. You may have to scroll to see the option.
3. Confirm App Install
Confirm adding the Impact Disciples App to your Home Screen. You may also adjust the app name in this step.
4. Open App
You should now see the Impact Disciples App within your app screen. It may look like the image pictured above.
Chrome
1. Click the Menu Screen button
The Menu button is typically in the top right corner of the screen. Look for the icon pictured above.
2. Add to Home Screen
The list of share options will have an 'Add to Home Screen' button. You may have to scroll to see the option.
3. Select Install
If provided the soption to "Install" or "Create A Shortcut", select "Install".
4. Confirm App Install
Confirm installing the Impact Disciples App to Phone.
5. Open App
You should now see the Impact Disciples App within your list of apps. It may look like the image pictured above.. You may have to search your app gallery.
